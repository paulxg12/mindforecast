How it works

Each (country, disorder) series is turned into lag-1..5 values plus rolling mean/std and a 5-year trend. One model is trained across all series, with the disorder passed as a categorical feature. The backtest holds out 2015-2019 and compares against a persistence baseline. The projection then feeds its own predictions back in, one year at a time, to reach 2030.
